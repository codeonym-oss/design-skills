# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, critique work rather than people, and keep
discussions on topic. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/codeonym-oss/design-skills/security/advisories/new)
(mark it "code of conduct") or by contacting [@codeonym](https://github.com/codeonym). Reports are handled
confidentially; maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban contributors as the Covenant describes.
